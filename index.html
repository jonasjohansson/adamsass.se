Adam Sass

info@adamsass.se
Malmö, Sweden

Adam Sass leads People In Orbit, an electroacoustic jazz group he writes for and directs. Their second album Viewpoint came out on April Records in 2026, after Close/Away in 2023.

He co-writes in floats, a seven-piece from Sweden and Norway that has made everything as a group since 2017, and plays with BEQ, Vidar Orchestra, Spontaneity Quartet, Isildurs Bane and Lina Nyberg. He has recorded with Per Gessle, Erik Wallander, Idyllia, Anton Liljedahl and many others. Alongside the bands he writes for larger ensembles: Is There A Garden In My Head? for big band, Sunrise, Meeting, Remembrance for string quartet and jazz ensemble, and music for the film series Framtidens Historia.

He studied at the Malmö Academy of Music with Tomasz Dąbrowski, Peter Asplund and Bobo Stenson. In 2025 he took second prize in Blåsmusikpriset Jazz and played 82 concerts in Sweden, Denmark, the Faroe Islands, Iceland and Germany. His work has been supported by Stim, Kungliga Musikaliska Akademien, Malmö Stad and Sten K Johnsons Stiftelse.

An impressive achievement which bodes well for the future of this potent outfit.
