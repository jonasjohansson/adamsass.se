About

Adam Sass leads the electroacoustic jazz group People In Orbit, where he writes the music and directs the band.

He has played with Lina Nyberg, floats, BEQ, Isildurs Bane and Vidar Orchestra, among many others. In 2025 he took second prize in Blåsmusikpriset Jazz, the Swedish award for best young jazz horn player. He studied at the Malmö Academy of Music, with trumpet players and musicians including Tomasz Dąbrowski, Peter Asplund and Bobo Stenson.

Alongside the bands he writes for larger ensembles: the big band piece Is There A Garden In My Head?, the suite Sunrise, Meeting, Remembrance for string quartet and jazz ensemble, and music for the film series Framtidens Historia. He plays 80 or more concerts a year: in 2025, 82 of them across 96 days in Sweden, Denmark, the Faroe Islands, Iceland and Germany.
